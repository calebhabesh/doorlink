The PCB row includes five fabricated boards with JLCPCB assembly; the ESP32-S3 and microphone were purchased separately. This is a build cost record, not a shopping list or a quote for a new order.

The thermistor and PIR are installed. PIR event wake is currently disabled in firmware and can be enabled with a firmware update. The subtotal uses unrounded unit allocations, so it can differ by a cent from adding the displayed rows.

Less than 5 cm of the 1 m silicone microphone sound pipe was used. At $3.83 per metre, its share is under $0.20 and is excluded from the subtotal because the exact length was not recorded.

Small assembly consumables and wiring, including screws, washers, connectors, adhesive, and speaker gasket material, are not priced here. The Raspberry Pi gateway, its power supply, and storage are also outside this **main enclosure** cost.
